# Recall web product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

Capture information without filing it, recover it from an incomplete recollection, and inspect the original evidence. Product authority remains ../../docs/PRD.md; architecture and delivery status remain in the canonical docs.

## Users and Operating Context

People who photograph handwritten notes and later need to recover details. The active pilot is responsive web on iPhone and Windows. Paul is a design partner, not the audience boundary.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Private workspace-scoped sources; original bytes saved before processing; honest local/upload/processing states; source-backed answers and uncertainty. No invented identities, verification, offline generative answers, or autonomous external actions. Live device and provider acceptance remain separate from UI work.

## Product Principles

- Capture before classification.
- Recover first; inspect evidence immediately.
- Preserve uncertainty and human corrections.
- Keep organizational complexity behind the interface.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing React capture, Ask, originals and authentication flows in src/App.tsx. Design illustrations use clearly labeled synthetic examples only.

## Design Brief

The user delegated the defining experience and visual choice. The selected defining moment is recovering the right source from a vague recollection.
